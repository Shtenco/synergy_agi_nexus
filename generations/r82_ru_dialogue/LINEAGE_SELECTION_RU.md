# NEXUS R8.2 — lineage selection matrix

Legend:

- **CORE** — sovereign or required runtime mechanism;
- **KEEP** — promote mechanism into R8.2;
- **TEACHER** — training/critique signal only;
- **ADVISORY** — optional evidence/surface organ without authority;
- **REGRESSION** — preserve test contract, not necessarily trainable implementation;
- **REJECT** — do not promote the claimed mechanism/result;
- **RESEARCH** — potentially useful but requires new evidence.

## Non-negotiable cross-generation rules

1. **Verified native proof cannot be overwritten by neural confidence or teacher consensus.**
2. **Correlated teachers count by independent lineage, not model count.**
3. **A large graph is not automatically factual truth.**
4. **Hidden/absent information -> UNKNOWN or Active Inquiry, not hallucinated completion.**
5. **The Russian surface generator may realize approved claims but may not create new facts.**
6. **Historical disclosed tests remain regressions, not new blinds.**
7. **Component 99–100% metrics do not imply open-domain dialogue quality.**
8. **Dialogue quality must be measured on fresh multi-turn Russian conversations after freeze.**
9. **No automatic financial/payment/ledger authority.**
10. **Raw V8 replay is not claimed until the downloaded archive is independently extracted and row/hash audited.**

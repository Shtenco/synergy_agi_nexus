# Federation V3 — NEXUS / Distributed Intelligence expansion

This repository implements the reasoning portion of historical **Part VIII — AI governance / «Россия-Разум»**, plus analytical support for Parts XIII–XV.

## Preserve from the origin concept

- distributed intelligence across local/regional/central nodes;
- continual learning from domain evidence;
- optimization and scenario analysis across finance, science, industry and public systems;
- human+AI collaboration;
- system-wide feedback reasoning.

## Modern NEXUS responsibilities

1. World-model construction from typed evidence.
2. Multi-domain causal and graph reasoning.
3. Scenario generation and sensitivity analysis.
4. Provenance tracking: `KNOWN / LEARNED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN`.
5. Explicit uncertainty and selective answering.
6. Policy proposals with assumptions and constraints.
7. Cross-domain dependency reasoning for household, business, finance, science, industry and state systems.
8. Scientific hypothesis support through `nexus_science_agent`.

## NEXUS must not own

- payment authority;
- signing authority;
- accounting authority;
- trading execution authority;
- merge/deployment authority;
- state/budget execution authority;
- blockchain submission authority by default.

## Output contract

Every consequential proposal should include:
- proposal ID;
- domain;
- objective;
- evidence references;
- assumptions;
- uncertainty;
- alternatives;
- constraints;
- expected impact;
- risk/failure modes;
- required approval/capability;
- expiry/freshness window.

NEXUS output flows to `agi_autopilot`, never directly to money movement or production control.

## Distributed-intelligence topology

Historical central/regional/local hierarchy becomes a federated topology:
`NEXUS reasoning core ↔ specialist agents ↔ regional/domain nodes ↔ SynergyNet ↔ Meta-OS`.

Node placement and runtime are owned by `synergy_meta_os`; transport by `synergynet`; authority by `synergy_system`.
